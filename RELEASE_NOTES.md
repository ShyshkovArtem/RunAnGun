# Run&Gun Pre-Alpha 0.2.0

This is an early public playtest of the Run&Gun vertical slice. It is intended to demonstrate the core movement, weapon-platforming, trial progression, and time-trial systems while gathering feedback before production expands into full delivery missions.

## Included in this build

- Six playable training courses: five mechanic-focused trials and a final licensing test
- Sprinting, jumping, crouching, sliding, momentum, checkpoints, and fall recovery
- Pistol, shotgun, rifle, and bazooka mechanics with weapon-driven movement
- Timed completion, persistent best times, ranks, and level-selection progress
- Reworked training dialogue and clearer Space Delivery Corps context
- Display, audio, control rebinding, mouse sensitivity, and crosshair settings
- English and Russian localization
- Updated trial environments, lighting, UI, audio balance, and gameplay feedback

## Known limitations

- This is a pre-alpha build; bugs, unfinished content, and balance issues are expected.
- The current UI is designed for 1920x1080. Other resolutions are not supported yet.
- Keyboard and mouse are the supported input method; controller support is not ready.
- Visuals, audio, level layouts, dialogue, saved progress, and system requirements may change.
- The game is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.

## Feedback

Please report issues through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ShyshkovArtem/RunAnGun/issues). Include reproduction steps, the affected menu or trial, your Windows version and hardware, and a screenshot or video when possible.

Thank you for testing Run&Gun.
